## About this entry

The brief wants one sentence that makes a small everyday moment strange
and new, staying grounded in something real, and the lift is the right
stage for it: a sealed metal box where strangers stand in silence and
anything can happen in a single floor. The wonder here is carried by one
impossible detail - a stranger humming the narrator's grandmother's
lullaby, a tune nobody in that lift should know. Nothing is explained,
and nothing needs to be; the sentence simply lets the coincidence sit
there until the last words turn the whole building into home for one
floor. It is one sentence, a hundred and twenty-two characters, and the
surprise lands in a single read. The program holds the sentence, checks
it against the two hundred character cap, and writes it to
/output/entry.txt exactly as the bounty requires.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the one-sentence entry to `/output/entry.txt`.
